# P0 — Art-First Spatial and Terrain Blueprint (DEFAULT)

Purpose: create an evidence-led editable spatial design for **Blender environment art**; playability is optional. Run P0-A then P0-B. Default successor: P1-concept. Only run P1-playable when requested explicitly.

Evidence priority: (1) original image and approved visual design, (2) authored landform/flow logic, (3) optional **user-supplied Tripo small 3D mesh** as an approximate visual/depth reference, (4) older P0 candidate data as documented hypotheses. Never mistake a generated asset for surveyed terrain, verified ground, metric scale, collision, or exact hidden geometry. Preserve historical source evidence and prior reports; write versioned revisions.

## P0-A — Analysis
Follow P0-A.md. Record visible landmarks, top-down scene hypothesis, terrace levels and cliff silhouettes, directional stream with distinct waterfall drops/pools, foreground/midground/background design, visual paths if useful, source and review cameras, confidence and ambiguity. Do not model yet.

## P0-B — Artifact generation
Follow P0-B.md. Create a valid 16-bit heightfield as bulk-design terrain, separate cliff and overhang boundaries, water graph, landform-zone masks, true top-down plan, camera shots, and a multi-scale **slope diagnostic**. Optional navigation graph is for future routing only.

## Distinct slope categories
There is **no universal natural-terrain maximum slope**. Gentle land, rolling hills, scree, vertical rock, riverbanks, waterfall lips and optional paths require different design rules. Inspect authored-heightfield slope at about 0.5, 2 and 5 meter smoothing scales. A design guide such as 18 degrees applies **only to a surface explicitly designated for future regular walking**, never to all natural terrain. A steep rock face or waterfall drop must use independent 3D geometry rather than a single-valued heightfield.

## Acceptance and scope
Check geometry consistency, natural nonuniform bank/cliff shapes, water stepping, landmark screen layout, provenance and reproducible output. Document actual PASS/FAIL/ASSUMED/NOT RUN. P0_ART_BLUEPRINT_APPROVED enables Blender P1-concept; no Godot runtime, CharacterBody3D, NavMesh, collision or gameplay evidence is required at this gate. No paid generation or replacement of originals.
