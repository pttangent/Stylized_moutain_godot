# P4 — Conditional scene systems and assembly

P4-A.md covers **only** procedural/simulated/derived-data systems actually needed by the approved scene. It may correctly return `NOT_APPLICABLE` and leave no simulation. P4-B.md assembles the selected scene from approved geometry, materials and assets.

Avoid assuming a particular biome, urban program, building type, animation, camera, renderer, route graph, fluid simulation or procedural placement strategy. Review-view selection and assembly strategy come from `scene_profile.json`. Art acceptance does not depend on P1-playable.
